Focused Task is a simple planner with a built-in focus mode. Plan your day, work through it one task at a time, and see where your time really went – without accounts, ads or tracking.

PLAN
• Put tasks on days, order them by drag and drop and break them into sub-tasks.
• Estimate how long a task takes; an alarm rings when the time is up.
• Group tasks into categories with their own colour, as a list or as a board.
• Week and month calendar to move tasks between days.
• Unfinished tasks from the last days can be moved to today with one tap.

FOCUS
• Focus mode shows one task at a time: its timer, its sub-tasks and what comes next.
• Optional breaks in the Pomodoro style: work for 25 minutes, then take a short break, with a longer one after every fourth round. All lengths are adjustable.
• Focus on a single category, skip a task for later, or add a sub-task without leaving focus.
• Park a stray thought with one line and stay on your task; review it afterwards and turn it into a task.
• A minimal layout shows only the timer and your task.
• Optionally pauses when you leave the app and keeps the screen awake.

TRACK
• Every minute of focused work is recorded, also across app switches, crashes and midnight.
• If the app was closed during a session, you decide whether to keep the time.
• Missing time can be added by hand.

REFLECT
• Statistics per day, week, month or year: focus time, completed tasks, planned vs. actual time, your best focus hours and top tasks.
• A daily focus goal for your working days, with streaks.
• Break time is shown separately and never counts as focus time.
• See how often you were interrupted per focus hour.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• All data stays on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Light and dark theme, English and German.
